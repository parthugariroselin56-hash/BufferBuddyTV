What can a Fire TV app detect?

SituationLikely to workWhy
Your own player inside the appYesAn HTML5 video fires waiting, stalled, error and playing events.
Device loses internetLikelyBrowsers expose online and offline events, and Fire OS has network-state APIs.
Buffering inside Netflix or Prime VideoNoApps are sandboxed, so another app’s player state is not visible without an official partner capability.

Check Amazon’s current Fire TV developer documentation for web app support and overlay rules before building further.
