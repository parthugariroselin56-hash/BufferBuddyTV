Make every wait worth watching.

BufferBuddy TV replaces spinners and error screens with short, lightweight animated moments whenever playback is interrupted. It does not fix your connection. It makes the wait easier to sit through.

What is inside

Interruption player

Trigger buffering, Wi-Fi loss, slow connection and error events. Includes auto-demo and remote-style keyboard control.

Buddy library

Four animated characters. Choose which buddy appears for each type of interruption.

Analytics

Every interruption is logged. See totals, averages, a seven-day chart and a CSV export.

Settings

Colour theme, animation speed, captions and auto-demo timing, saved on the device.

Architecture and feasibility

How the code is organised, and what a Fire TV app can and cannot detect.

An honest limit

A regular app cannot see buffering inside Netflix or Prime Video. BufferBuddy works with signals it can really observe: its own player, and the device going offline.
